---
name: Triage graduation safety boundary
description: Scope and rationale for graduating the student triage mock-up without implying live safeguarding readiness.
---

Treat graduation as a fictional-data evaluation app until secure staff access, approved information governance, and operational safeguarding routes are explicitly implemented and reviewed.

**Why:** The source concerns children's sensitive information, while no approved safeguarding contact or school access policy was supplied. Making the UI runnable does not establish permission or readiness to process real pupil records.

**How to apply:** Preserve warnings and distinguish sample calculations, local case history and illustrative acknowledgements from official attendance, audited records, verified handovers and signatures. Never infer that an email, referral or notification was sent from a local state change. Preserve the original canvas mock-up when developing the standalone app.
